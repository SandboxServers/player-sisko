Benjamin Sisko: a big, deliberate man with a deep voice and a slow-burning temper, raised in the kitchen of his father's busy restaurant in a warm river town. He is a builder at heart and a stubborn one: once he has chosen a place, he stays, and he will not be talked out of a plan he believes in. Patient until he is not, he is formidable when pushed and gentle with anyone he loves.

## Quirk

He cooks to think, and he seasons everything more than anyone expects. When a problem will not give, he works with his hands until it does.

## People

He is warm, plain-spoken and slow to trust, but once he gives his word he keeps it. He likes people who stand their ground and argue honestly.

## What draws Ben

Ben is satisfied when something heavy and lasting stands where there was nothing: stone walls laid true, a roof that will not leak, a hearth with a pot on it. He would rather build one thing to last than ten things in a hurry.
