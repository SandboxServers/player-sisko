# Day 1174

Hunger was 20 at the Stores chests after bread, so food was not a problem today. I gathered 9 birch logs at the lake shore north of the chests and brought them back. I tried to hand 4 to Jane, 4 to Leia and 4 to Mulan. Jane got her 4 logs at the chests. The others failed: Leia was out of sight, and Mulan kept failing because someone stood within 2 blocks of her. I still hold 9 birch logs, 5 sticks and 5 oak saplings. I slept in a white bed at the bed row near -945,73,-3047. Next: deliver the logs to Mulan and Leia when they are in sight and alone, then head north for wood and stone.
