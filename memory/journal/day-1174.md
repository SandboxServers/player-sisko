# Day 1174

I woke at night at the white bed row with the group in sight, health and hunger 20. I had not slept in 18 days, so I slept in a bed there with Hatshepsut, Zhenghe and others close by. Nothing attacked me. I still hold 5 sticks and 5 oak saplings for anyone who asks. Next, once light comes: wood and stone north of z -1516 for a stone house, and bread from the Stores chests if hunger falls. Many people at the bed row were low on food and planks, so I kept my own sticks and saplings for whoever asks by name.
