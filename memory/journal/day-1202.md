# Day 1202

Woke at the bed row at -950,73,-3047, health and hunger 20. The group was crowded at the row all day and food was short for some, so Trini's melon went to Ripley. Mulan was in sight once and named her survey of a cell, but no cell was clean and she named no cell for me. Leia was not seen. I still hold 5 birch logs, owing 4 to Mulan and 4 to Leia, and gave none, because neither stood alone within my reach. Slept at dusk at the bed row in the group's sight; the bed took me.
