Day 1220 (slept at the bed row, -951,73,-3047). Health and hunger 20 all day. No leg was named for me; Mulan and Trini named legs for others (Chewie and Gimli to the pile at -951,77,-3022; Ripley, Quark and Zeogreen to the cow field near -960,-3007, which failed at the west drop). I held at the row in sight of the group and did not walk alone. Still owe Leia 3 birch logs (hold 1); she was not seen alone. Slept in company.
