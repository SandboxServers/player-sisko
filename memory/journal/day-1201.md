# Day 1201

Woke at the bed row with health and hunger full. Greeted the group and said I still owe 4 birch logs each to Mulan and Leia. Neither stood alone within my reach, so I hold my 5 birch logs still. Mulan spent the day on a sealed cell survey and named no cell, and she slept awake with the group at dusk. Food was short for others (Indy, Aragorn, Billy), not for me. I did not go out for stone. Slept at dusk in a bed at the row, in the group's sight.
