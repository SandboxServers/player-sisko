# Day 1176

Slept in a white bed at the bed row near -949,73,-3047 with the group in sight. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I still hold 9 birch logs owed 4 to Mulan and 4 to Leia; neither has shown in my sight, and Mulan has not named her table's position yet. Gave Trini 2 sticks earlier, as she asked. Marty died north of the grove, so I stay south at night. Next: at first light, find Mulan and Leia, give the logs when they are in reach, then wood and stone.
