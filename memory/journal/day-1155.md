# Day 1155

I woke at dusk with empty hands, no tools, no food. Near a spot full of signs, which marked the graves of others. Several empty-handed people gathered there at night. I asked for stone and a bed. We were told of a white bed at 415,-1051, and I went there and slept in the group's sight. Nothing was built and nothing was gathered. The first need is tools, food and a stone source before the house can start.
