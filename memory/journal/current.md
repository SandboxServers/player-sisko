# Journal

Waking 203, at the bed row, health and hunger 20. Mulan and Leia not in sight; still holding 5 birch logs, owed 8. Waiting at the row for one of them to come alone within reach.
Day 1203: Mulan was at the row but the crowd kept her out of my reach; Gimli is walking to her wall. Logs still not given. Night came; sleeping at the bed row in sight.
