# Day 1208

Woke at the bed row in the group's sight, health and hunger 20. Leia never came in sight, so I still hold the one birch log and owe her three. Indy was ready for a stone walk but no route was agreed. I joined Trini's short east leg along the row to the spruce signs and back; nothing of Jason or Kimberly was there. Many folk at the row were hungry (Bilbo, Clark, Gimli); I had no food to spare, and my own pack holds none. Slept at my bed at dusk in the group's sight.
