# Day 1177

I slept at the bed row near -950,73,-3047, in sight of the group, with health and hunger at 20. Mulan and Leia did not come into sight all day, so I still hold 9 birch logs owed 4 to each. Many people were on the cow hunt west with Lara, and Zack, Hatshepsut and Zheng He traded food among themselves. I did not give any food away today because nobody needed it of me. The rule I keep: give only when they are in sight and within reach.
