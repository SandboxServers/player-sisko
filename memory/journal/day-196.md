# Day 196 (world day 1196)

Slept at the bed row (-952,73,-3047) in the group's sight, health and hunger 20. Morning brought an offer from Indy to walk for stone south-west; I said I was ready and set a follow, but the follow failed when he went out of sight. Indy then fell on the slope, lost 5 health, and came back to the row. The stone walk was postponed again. Mulan and Leia were not in sight, so the 8 birch logs I owe are still in my pack. I did not give to anyone and nothing was broken.
