# Day 1197

Woke at the bed row in the group's sight, health and hunger 20. Mulan named herself alone and I walked to her with 4 birch logs, but Tubman and Seven stood within 2 blocks, so the give failed and the logs stayed with me. Mulan and Leia are still owed 8 logs in total; I still hold 5. Indy's stone walk did not happen. Slept at dusk at the bed row, in sight of the group. Lesson: a give waits on a clear moment, not on a name.
